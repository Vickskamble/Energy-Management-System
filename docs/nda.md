> This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement governs the exchange of confidential information between the parties in connection with the evaluation, negotiation, onboarding, licensing, or investment in the PowerEMS Energy Management System.

---

## 1. Purpose

The parties wish to exchange confidential information for the purpose of (the **"Purpose"**): evaluating and negotiating a potential business relationship, including but not limited to: product demonstration and evaluation, client onboarding and tariff configuration, subscription/licensing terms, investment or partnership discussions, security and technical due diligence.

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

**"Confidential Information"** means any non-public information disclosed by either party, whether oral, written, or electronic, including but not limited to:

- **PowerEMS (Disclosing Party):** source code, algorithms, billing/formula logic, architecture, security controls, test data, roadmap, financials, pricing, customer lists, and trade secrets.
- **Client/Investor (Disclosing Party):** business plans, financials, operational data, factory/meter data, electricity consumption records, tariff arrangements, and any data uploaded or demonstrated during onboarding.

Confidential Information includes all notes, analyses, and materials derived from it. It does **not** include information that: (a) is or becomes public through no breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully in the Receiving Party's possession before disclosure; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without obligation of confidence; or (d) is independently developed without use of the disclosed information.

## 3. Obligations of the Receiving Party

The Receiving Party shall:

1. Use Confidential Information **only for the Purpose** and for no other commercial or personal benefit.
2. Protect it with **reasonable care** (at least the care used for its own confidential information) — for PowerEMS data, at least the technical and organizational measures described in `security-overview.md`.
3. Disclose it **only to personnel with a need to know** for the Purpose, bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Agreement.
4. **Not copy, reverse-engineer, decompile, or sublicense** the other party's software/source code.
5. Return or destroy Confidential Information (and copies) on request or termination of discussions, certifying destruction in writing where requested.

## 4. Permitted Disclosures

The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information:

1. To the extent **required by law**, court order, or regulator — provided it gives prompt written notice (where lawful) so the Disclosing Party can seek protection, and discloses only the minimum required.
2. To its **professional advisers** (legal, accounting) bound by equivalent confidentiality.
3. For PowerEMS: personal data is additionally governed by the Privacy Policy and DPA — nothing in this NDA permits processing of client data beyond the DPA terms.

## 5. Duration & Survival

- The obligations apply during discussions and for **5 years** after the last disclosure (trade secrets: for as long as they remain trade secrets).
- On termination of any underlying business relationship, this NDA survives.

## 6. No License or Warranty

- No license, ownership, or rights are granted in the other party's Confidential Information or IP by disclosure.
- All Confidential Information is provided **"as is"** — without warranties of accuracy, completeness, or fitness for purpose.

## 7. Breach & Remedies

- Breach may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages are inadequate; the non-breaching party is entitled to **injunctive relief** in addition to any other remedies.
- The breaching party shall indemnify the non-breaching party for losses caused by the breach (subject to applicable law).

## 8. No Obligation to Proceed

Nothing obligates either party to enter into any transaction. Either party may terminate discussions at any time without liability.

## 9. Governing Law & Disputes

- Governed by the laws of **India**; courts of [city/state — to be filled] have exclusive jurisdiction.
- The parties shall first attempt good-faith resolution; then arbitration in accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, before resorting to courts.

## 10. General

- This Agreement is the **entire agreement** on confidentiality between the parties and supersedes prior discussions.
- Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.
- No third-party beneficiaries, except as expressly stated.
- If any provision is unenforceable, the remainder stays in effect.
